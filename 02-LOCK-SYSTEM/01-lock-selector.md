## The Selector — Four Questions

1. **Is a real person in it?** → **Identity**
2. **Is there a physical thing being sold?** → **Product.** Is it an experience instead? → **Place + Claim**
3. **Are you inside someone else's brand rules?** → **Brand System**
4. **Are shots made separately, or does a person recur across scenes?** → **Continuity**, and **Character** if fictional
