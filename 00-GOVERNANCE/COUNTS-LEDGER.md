## Status definitions

- **Attested** — counted during a working session against the live draft, confirmed at the time, recorded here with its date. Not re-checkable from this repository.
- **Verified** — counted against a file committed to this repository. Re-checkable by anyone.

## Why the distinction holds

The content counts were genuinely performed and genuinely confirmed. They are recorded here as made, not treated as guesses.

But they were counted against drafts that do not yet exist in this repository. Until the files land here, no one reviewing this repo can re-run those counts. Promoting them to Verified before that point would make the ledger claim something it cannot show.

Each row moves to Verified, with a new date, on the commit that brings its source file in.

## Standing rule

No figure is repeated or certified from memory. It is counted against the file, or it is marked Attested with its date and origin.
