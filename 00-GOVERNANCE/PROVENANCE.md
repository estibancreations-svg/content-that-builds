# Provenance

## Origin of the source material

The original *Content That Builds* document was produced inside a small group — Rev. Dr. Christian Frazier, Stephen / Estiban Creations, and two others.

The group shared what each member had learned. Christian then assembled and organized that shared material into the document, for the use of everyone in the group. No member claimed exclusive ownership of it, and it was given freely among the participants.

## Terms of use in this repository

- **Non-commercial.** This Second Edition is not for sale and is not intended for sale.
- **Internal.** It is used to advance the systems, processes, and production pipelines of Estiban Creations.
- **Attributed.** Christian's authorship of the organized document stands, and the credit line is retained on this repository.
- **Private.** This repository is private. Any change to that is a decision to be made jointly, not unilaterally.
