## How to Use This Workbook

This is a universal production system for AI content creation and marketing. The examples inside — a restaurant, a cruise line, a camera launch, a film scene — are **illustrations, not scope limits**. The same spine applies to automotive campaigns, consumer electronics, travel packages, nonprofits, and narrative film production.

**Start at the Diagnostic Router**, not at Chapter 1. Find the sentence closest to the problem in front of you right now, and go to the page it points to. The chapters teach the parts; the Router teaches you which part you need today.

If something goes wrong and you can't name why, go to **Chapter 10.5 — The Drift Ladder**. Name the symptom first. The cause is almost never what it looks like.

If your job has no face and no product — an experience, a place, a promise — go to the **Lock Family** and use the four-question selector to find which locks your job actually needs.

If you're producing a story rather than an ad, go to **Narrative Mode**. It's the same machine with a different beat map.

Read **Chapter 13** to see the whole system run three times, on three unrelated jobs, side by side. That's the fastest way to understand that the process is the product — not any one example in it.
