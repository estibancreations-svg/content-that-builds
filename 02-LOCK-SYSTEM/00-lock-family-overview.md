# The Lock Family

*Replaces "Reference lock," Chapter 3.*

**The principle never changes:** the tool remembers nothing. Whatever must not change gets written into every prompt, every scene, every time.

**What changes:** two slots become eight. Use only the ones your job has.

| Lock | Use when | Protects |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | A real person appears | Face, skin tone, age, hair, eyewear, facial hair, build, natural asymmetry |
| **Product** | A physical object is sold | Shape, materials, color, label placement, logo, ports, scale, packaging |
| **Place** | A real location is shown | Architecture, signage, layout, street logic, landmarks, time of day |
| **Claim** | Facts are stated | Price, dates, inclusions, exclusions, specs, results, availability |
| **Brand System** | Inside a brand's rules | Approved colors, badge, type, clear space, disclosure, forbidden depictions |
| **Character** | A fictional person recurs | Design, wardrobe, props, marks, apparent age at story point |
| **Continuity** | Shots generated separately | Wardrobe state, prop position, light direction, screen direction, eyeline |
| **Copy** | Words appear on screen | Spelling, line breaks, numbers, CTA, legal text |

**Character** and **Continuity** exist specifically to support filmmaking — they carry a story point instead of being static, because a character's wardrobe or prop state changes as the story moves.

**Continuity rule, unchanged and absolute:** repeat the full lock in every scene prompt. Never rely on the tool to remember a previous generation.

See `01-lock-selector.md` for the four-question selector and `02-stacking-table-by-job-type.md` for which combinations a real job uses. Filled examples live below the blanks in each template in `templates/` — the blank comes first, the filled example second, as proof the slot works rather than as the slot itself.

> **Identity, filled example:** …preserve facial structure, skin tone, age, bald head, black glasses, salt-and-pepper goatee and mustache, height, slim athletic build, wardrobe, and natural proportions…

> **Claim, filled example:** Verified and locked: 7-night Western Caribbean, $1,249 per person double occupancy, sailing Nov 8–15, includes balcony stateroom, main dining, port fees. Excludes airfare, specialty dining, excursions, gratuities. Do not depict a suite, a specialty restaurant, or an excursion.
